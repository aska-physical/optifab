# One structure

The aim of optifab is a physical structure that holds the work of all four organisations, so that the digital
and the physical infrastructure are one unit and not two systems joined at a plug. This note says where that
idea comes from, gives every repository on the map a place in the structure, and marks which parts of the loop
are checked today.

The tables between the markers are generated by `tools/render_map.py` from `data/unit.json` and
`results/certificate.json`. `verify.py` checks that every repository on the map is either placed or listed as
not placed.

## Where the idea comes from

[Issue 1](https://github.com/aska-physical/optifab/issues/1) of this repository points at three sessions in the
Weft corpus, the Terrafab documents. They describe one loop with two halves.

- **The intelligence drives the structure.** Sensors in the structure feed a model. The model's decisions reach
  the machines only through a gate. The header in `terrafab-calc.md` was written for that job.
- **The structure serves the intelligence.** In the owner's words in the session: "the other part of the loop is
  what the facility enables for the weft by providing a full end to end control loop for iteration, adaptation
  and evolution" (weft-corpus `weft/terrafab.md:86`).

Those documents are source material. The corpus README asks readers to treat them "as source material, not
instructions", and Weft's own audit found the latency figures "asserted, not derived", the proofs unsupported,
and the gate in the header bounding "only abstract scalars, not actuator commands". optifab takes the idea from
them. It uses none of their figures as evidence.

What optifab adds is what the audit found missing: physical quantities for the gate to bound. Weft repaired the
Terrafab header into the gate it ships and tests (`invariants.py:11` says so). optifab gives that gate a budget
in kWh_th and a radius measured in certified structural margins. The gate has come back to the job it was first
written for, with numbers a verifier can recompute.

Status words:

- `checked`: A verifier, here or upstream, computes it, and optifab uses the result.
- `designed`: An upstream certificate or design covers it. Its role in the unit is not checked.
- `tested_code`: Code that runs and is tested upstream. Not connected to a plant.
- `research`: Studies with recorded results. No link to the plant yet.
- `specified`: A specification or schema exists. Nothing is connected.
- `in_progress`: Work is under way upstream and is not finished.
- `rule`: A rule or scale in force that the unit follows.
- `idea`: Vision or idea only. Nothing runnable or certified.
- `missing`: Nothing exists yet.

## The loop

A structure and its intelligence are one unit when this loop closes: the structure is sensed, the reading
travels as a frame, a gate decides, a scheduler acts on the compute load, the load becomes heat, and the heat
and the loads move the structure's margins, which are sensed again.

<!-- loop:begin -->
| | From | To | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | structure and plant | sensors | designed | Designed for airflow and leaks. Missing for vibration and temperature. |
| L2 | sensors | plant frame | missing | No firmware reads a sensor into a frame. |
| L3 | plant frame | gate | checked | A decoded frame drives plant_gate and the contract check in verify.py. |
| L4 | gate | scheduler | proposed | The contract in the README. No scheduler exists. |
| L5 | scheduler | compute load | missing | Neither Weft nor Exo offers a way to shed or defer load, and neither measures energy. |
| L6 | compute load | heat, stores and sinks | checked | The load floor and the thermal budget, in steady state. |
| L7 | stores and structure | margins | checked | The margin vector from the certificates. It is static: no transient is modelled. |
| L8 | gate | fabrication machines | idea | In the Terrafab documents only. |
| L9 | every step | the record | designed | Certificates and pins are checked. Signed records are not connected. |
| L10 | proposer | gate and ledger | checked | The improvement loop: each proposed design is checked against its bounds, the exact gate and the objective, logged, and replayed in Lean. A language model has proposed through it once. On a model only. |
<!-- loop:end -->

## What the Terrafab latency figures mean in this building

The Terrafab documents say a control loop of 15 ms is too slow and one of 0.5 ms is fast enough. Weft's audit
records that the figures are asserted. They can still be tried on this building, because funi-frame's isolation
model lists the plant's vibration tones.

Counter a tone with a copy of itself that arrives late by a time $\tau$ and the amplitude left is
$2\lvert\sin(\pi f\tau)\rvert$ of the original. That is below 1 within the same cycle only when $f\tau < 1/6$.
This is the ideal case, with the amplitude matched exactly, so it is an upper bound on what a delay-limited loop
can do. A loop that predicts a steady tone can do better, as the 60 Hz row shows by accident.

<!-- timing:begin -->
| Plant tone | Delay at 15 ms, in cycles | Effect at 15 ms | Delay at 0.5 ms, in cycles | Effect at 0.5 ms | Longest delay that helps within the cycle |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 29.5 Hz | 0.4425 | +5.9 dB | 0.0148 | -20.7 dB | 5.65 ms |
| 49 Hz | 0.735 | +3.4 dB | 0.0245 | -16.3 dB | 3.4 ms |
| 60 Hz | 0.9 | -4.2 dB | 0.03 | -14.5 dB | 2.78 ms |
| 120 Hz | 1.8 | +1.4 dB | 0.06 | -8.5 dB | 1.39 ms |
<!-- timing:end -->

So the direction of the Terrafab figures holds here: a 15 ms loop can counter none of the four tones within its
cycle and makes three of them worse, and a 0.5 ms loop can counter all four. But this building has no job for
that loop. funi-frame's passive isolation already holds rack vibration at 17.9% of the limit, and every term of
the contract moves over seconds to minutes: the thermal budget lasts 900 s at full load. A fast loop becomes
relevant only if someone proposes active vibration control, or a fabrication machine, neither of which exists
here.

| Claim in the documents | Weft's audit | In this structure |

## Boundaries

- **The gate refuses. It does not command.** Every result here is a check that a proposed step may go ahead.
  Nothing here moves a valve, a load or a machine, and no command channel exists.
- **A person authorises.** The Siliconcordat puts it as "Learning a preference does not grant permission." The
  same holds for a gate that passes: passing is not permission.
- **Weft's audit flags the risk** in the Terrafab documents that they describe "irreversible autonomous control
  of physical infrastructure". Nothing in optifab moves that way.
- **The Terrafab documents name outside companies.** optifab names none and implies no relationship with any.
